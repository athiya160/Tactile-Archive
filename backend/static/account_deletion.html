Account & Data Deletion Request

The Tactile Archive (LibriFlow) • Google Play Data Safety Compliance
Important Notice: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. All personal information, reading logs, student schedules, and wishlists will be immediately erased.
1. How to Delete In-App (Instant)

The fastest way to delete your account is directly within the mobile application:

  1. Open The Tactile Archive mobile application.
  2. Navigate to Profile → Account Settings.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone.
  4. Tap Delete Account, verify your password (or type 'DELETE' for Google Sign-In), and confirm.
2. Data Deleted vs. Data Retained
  • Data Permanently Erased: Your name, email, phone number, saved delivery addresses, profile photo, reading history, student schedules, reading goals, favorites, and cart items are immediately purged.
  • Provider Book Listings: All active marketplace book listings belonging to your account are immediately archived and removed from search.
  • Active Fulfillment Safeguard: Accounts with active unreturned book rentals, active peer borrowings, or in-transit delivery orders cannot be deleted until all pending handoffs or returns are resolved.
  • Statutory Tax & Audit Retention: In compliance with statutory commercial taxation and accounting laws, past completed transaction invoices and payment records are retained in a strictly anonymized form with all recipient personal identifying information permanently scrubbed ([Deleted User], [Redacted - Account Deleted]).
